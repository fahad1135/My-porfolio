About Me

Behind the Frame

I'm Fahad Majeed — a video editor and content creator based in Pakistan with a passion for visual storytelling. With years of experience cutting everything from fast-paced social media content to long-form documentaries, I bring a sharp eye for pacing, emotion, and narrative clarity to every project I touch.
